Quick Tutorial

Your bike. Your part. Your best next move.

Here’s how to find your way around Bagger McGee.

1 Select→ 2 Compare→ 3 Check it out
Step One

Start with your bike.

Choose your make, year, and model, then hit FIND PARTS. Use the filters to narrow the list by category, retailer, condition, price, or shipping score.

Just looking? You can browse the opening list before choosing a bike.

Your ride comes first
Step Two

Read the whole offer.

Match the part number and details. Check NEW or USED, and OEM or AFTERMARKET. Sort by CONDITION, PRICE, or SHIP SCORE using the column headings.

The lowest item price may not mean the lowest total. Shipping and handling matter, too.

Step Three

Take it to the retailer.

Select the retailer’s name to explore the offer. Before buying, confirm fitment, availability, shipping charges, and the return policy with the retailer.

Bagger McGee helps you compare. Your purchase and fulfillment are handled by the retailer.

Before you buy

Bagger McGee finds the part and the price. You confirm the details on the retailer’s own website.

✓

Right part, right bike – confirm fitment on the retailer’s site.

✓

Item price + shipping + tax – confirm at the retailer’s checkout.

✓

Stock and return policy – check both on the retailer’s site.
